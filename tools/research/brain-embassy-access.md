# Building accessibility: Brain Embassy, Adgar 360 (HaShlosha 2, Tel Aviv)

Research date: 2026-10-24. Sources were read online only. Nobody was contacted and no forms were submitted.

## Bottom line

- The building owner (Adgar) states on its own Hebrew property page that the building has an accessible entrance, accessible parking, accessible restrooms and a reception desk with a hearing-assistance system. That is the strongest source, but it is a one-line list with no detail per entrance or floor.
- Brain Embassy's own Tel Aviv page says the building is "מונגש לבעלי מוגבלויות" and lists a "Disability adapted workspace". It gives no detail.
- The most detailed description comes from a third party in the same building: the German Embassy (HaShlosha 2, building section C). It describes a ramp, a step-free route through the underground garage and its elevator, and accessible parking. It covers the embassy's own entrance, not necessarily the lobby Brain Embassy guests use.
- Nothing found on: the elevators to floor 17 (size, voice, braille), restrooms on floor 17 or the ground floor specifically, entrance A, the "Mercedes" hall, guide dogs, a named accessibility coordinator for the building, or the walking route from the stations.

## What can go into the statement now (with sources)

1. The building has an accessible entrance, accessible parking, accessible restrooms and a reception desk with a hearing-assistance system (per the owner, Adgar).
2. The meetings are on floor 17, reached by elevator.
3. Accessible parking: in the building's underground garage and in the Menora Mivtachim arena lots (67 accessible spaces around the arena, per the arena operator).
4. A free shuttle runs from HaShalom train station.

## What to ask the venue before publishing

Ask Brain Embassy (03-7618824) or the building management (Ramot):
- Which entrance (A / C) is step-free, and where the accessible route from the parking garage arrives.
- Elevator to floor 17: voice announcements, braille buttons, cab size.
- Accessible restroom on floor 17 and near the Mercedes hall.
- Is the Mercedes hall reachable without steps? Is there a hearing loop there?
- Is the HaShalom shuttle wheelchair accessible?
- Name and phone of an accessibility contact for the building, for the statement.
